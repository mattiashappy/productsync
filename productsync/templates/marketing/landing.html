Paid orders are sacred.

Most "two-way" sync tools let dashboard edits silently overwrite a paid order. ProductSync doesn't.

Each field has an explicit owner. Real orders, inventory, and stock decrements always flow from your channels. Content, images, and prices always flow from your dashboard. No conflict resolution screens. No data loss.

Sync direction by field

  • Title, description, images Dashboard → channels
  • Price, compare-at price Dashboard → channels
  • Inventory quantity Channels → dashboard
  • Paid orders Channels → dashboard (append-only)
